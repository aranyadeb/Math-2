Q: If independent repeated trials (binomial) are involved
R: "Team plays 10 matches with $P(\text{win})=\frac13$", "sum and product of mean and variance are 24 and 128", "at least two hits needed; minimum number of bombs for 99%", "$3P(X=0)=P(X=1)$".
F: P(X=r)=\binom nrp^rq^{n-r},\quad\text{mean}=np,\quad\text{variance}=npq
S: Identify $n$, $p$ and what "success" means. || Write the required probability with the formula ("at least $k$" $=1-P(X<k)$). || From mean and variance: $q=\frac{npq}{np}$. || For ratios like $\frac{P(X=r)}{P(X=r+1)}$, use $\frac{\binom nr}{\binom n{r+1}}\cdot\frac qp=\frac{r+1}{n-r}\cdot\frac qp$. || For "minimum $n$", test values until the inequality holds.
E: $np$, $npq$ are roots of $t^2-24t+128=0$: $16$ and $8$ ⇒ $p=\frac12$, $n=32$.  Also: Bombs: need $1-\frac{n+1}{2^n}\ge0.99$ ⇒ $n=11$.

Q: If Bayes' theorem / total probability is needed (which bag, which city, which card is missing)
R: "Letter from KANPUR or ANANTPUR, 'AN' visible", "one card missing; two spades drawn; missing card not a spade", "ball transferred from bag I to bag II", "die decides how many balls are drawn".
F: P(E_i\mid A)=\frac{P(E_i)P(A\mid E_i)}{\sum_jP(E_j)P(A\mid E_j)}
S: List the possible causes $E_1,E_2,\ldots$ (they cover everything and don't overlap) with their prior probabilities. || For each, compute $P(A\mid E_i)$ — the chance of the observation if that cause happened. || Total probability $P(A)=\sum P(E_i)P(A\mid E_i)$. || Bayes: divide the required cause's term by the total.
E: $P(AN\mid K)=\frac15$, $P(AN\mid A)=\frac27$: $P(K\mid AN)=\frac{1/5}{1/5+2/7}=\frac7{17}$.  Also: Missing card: $\frac{39\cdot78}{13\cdot66+39\cdot78}=\frac{39}{50}$.
