## Sets and relations
Q: Cardinality of union
F: n(A\cup B)=n(A)+n(B)-n(A\cap B)
N: Three sets: $n(A\cup B\cup C)=\sum n(A)-\sum n(A\cap B)+n(A\cap B\cap C)$.

Q: De Morgan's laws
F: (A\cup B)'=A'\cap B',\qquad(A\cap B)'=A'\cup B'

Q: Distributive laws and differences
F: A\cap(B\cup C)=(A\cap B)\cup(A\cap C);\qquad A-B=A\cap B'

Q: Number of subsets and relations
F: |P(A)|=2^n;\qquad\#\text{relations } A\to B=2^{mn}
N: Reflexive relations on an $n$-set: $2^{n^2-n}$; symmetric: $2^{\frac{n(n+1)}2}$.

Q: Types of relations
F: \text{reflexive: }(a,a)\in R;\ \text{symmetric: }(a,b)\Rightarrow(b,a);\ \text{transitive: }(a,b),(b,c)\Rightarrow(a,c)
N: Equivalence = all three; it partitions the set into equivalence classes.

## Function basics
Q: Number of functions from A to B
F: |A|=m,\ |B|=n:\ \text{functions}=n^m,\quad\text{one-one}={}^nP_m\ (m\le n)
N: Onto ($m\ge n$): $\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^k\binom nk(n-k)^m$. Bijections when $m=n$: $n!$.

Q: Test for one-one and onto
F: f'(x)\ \text{of constant sign}\Rightarrow\text{one-one};\qquad\text{onto}\iff\text{range}=\text{codomain}
N: Horizontal line test.

Q: Even and odd functions
F: f(-x)=f(x)\ (\text{even, symmetric about }y\text{-axis}),\qquad f(-x)=-f(x)\ (\text{odd, about origin})
N: Every function $=\frac{f(x)+f(-x)}2+\frac{f(x)-f(-x)}2$.

Q: Periodic functions
F: f(x+T)=f(x);\quad\text{period of } f(ax+b)=\frac T{|a|}
N: $\sin^n x,\cos^n x$: period $\pi$ for $n$ even, $2\pi$ for $n$ odd; $|\sin x|+|\cos x|$: $\frac\pi2$. Period of a sum = LCM of periods (if it exists). $\{x\}$: period $1$.

Q: Composition and inverse
F: (f\circ g)(x)=f(g(x));\qquad f^{-1}\ \text{exists}\iff f\ \text{bijective};\quad(f\circ g)^{-1}=g^{-1}\circ f^{-1}
N: Graph of $f^{-1}$ is the mirror of $f$ in $y=x$; $f$ and $f^{-1}$ meet on $y=x$ when $f$ is increasing.

Q: Domain rules
F: \sqrt{f}:f\ge0;\quad\frac1f:f\ne0;\quad\log f:f>0;\quad\sin^{-1}f:|f|\le1

## Standard graphs
Q: Modulus function
F: y=|x|
G: y=abs(x) ; -4,4,-1,4

Q: Greatest integer function
F: y=[x],\ \text{discontinuous at integers}
G: y=floor(x) ; -3.5,3.5,-3.5,3.5

Q: Fractional part function
F: y=\{x\}=x-[x]\in[0,1)
G: y=x-floor(x) ; -3,3,-0.5,1.5

Q: Signum function
F: \operatorname{sgn}x=\begin{cases}1,&x>0\\0,&x=0\\-1,&x<0\end{cases}
G: y=sign(x) ; -3,3,-1.6,1.6

Q: Exponential and logarithm
F: y=e^x\ (\text{range }(0,\infty)),\qquad y=\ln x\ (\text{domain }(0,\infty))
G: y=exp(x) | y=log(x) | y=x ; -3,4,-3,4

Q: Polynomial and reciprocal curves
F: y=x^2,\quad y=x^3,\quad y=\frac1x,\quad y=\frac1{x^2}
G: y=x**2 | y=x**3 | y=1/x ; -3,3,-3,3

Q: Square root and cube root
F: y=\sqrt x\ (x\ge0),\qquad y=\sqrt[3]x\ (x\in\mathbb R)
G: y=sqrt(x) | y=cbrt(x) ; -4,4,-2,2.5

Q: Transformations of graphs
F: y=f(x)+k\ (\text{up}),\ f(x-h)\ (\text{right}),\ -f(x)\ (\text{flip in }x\text{-axis}),\ f(-x)\ (\text{flip in }y\text{-axis}),\ f(ax)\ (\text{squeeze})
N: $|f(x)|$: reflect the negative part up. $f(|x|)$: keep the right half, mirror it to the left.
G: y=sin(x) | y=abs(sin(x)) | y=sin(abs(x)) ; -6.5,6.5,-1.5,1.5

## Common results
Q: Functional equations
F: f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)\Rightarrow f(x)=kx;\quad f(xy)=f(x)+f(y)\Rightarrow k\ln x;\quad f(x+y)=f(x)f(y)\Rightarrow a^x
N: $f(x)+f\left(\frac1x\right)=f(x)f\left(\frac1x\right)\Rightarrow f(x)=1\pm x^n$.

Q: Max and min functions
F: \max(a,b)=\frac{a+b+|a-b|}2,\qquad\min(a,b)=\frac{a+b-|a-b|}2

Q: Range by AM–GM, quadratic in $x$, or monotonicity
F: y=f(x)\Rightarrow\text{solve for } x,\ \text{require real } x

Q: $f(x)=f^{-1}(x)$ shortcut
F: f\ \text{increasing}:\ f(x)=f^{-1}(x)\iff f(x)=x
