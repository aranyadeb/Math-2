## Continuity
Q: Continuity at a point
F: \lim_{x\to a^-}f(x)=\lim_{x\to a^+}f(x)=f(a)

Q: Types of discontinuity
F: \text{removable (limit exists} \ne f(a)\text{ or } f(a)\text{ undefined)};\ \text{jump};\ \text{infinite};\ \text{oscillatory}
N: Jump = $|f(a^+)-f(a^-)|$.

Q: Intermediate value theorem
F: f\ \text{continuous on }[a,b],\ f(a)f(b)<0\ \Rightarrow\ \exists c\in(a,b):\ f(c)=0

Q: Algebra of continuous functions
F: f,g\ \text{continuous}\Rightarrow f\pm g,\ fg,\ \tfrac fg\ (g\ne0),\ f\circ g\ \text{continuous}

## Differentiability
Q: Derivative from first principles
F: f'(a)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)}h
N: Left derivative $\lim_{h\to0^-}$, right derivative $\lim_{h\to0^+}$; differentiable iff both are equal and finite.

Q: Differentiable implies continuous
F: \text{differentiable}\Rightarrow\text{continuous};\ \text{converse false (e.g. } |x| \text{ at } 0)

Q: Sharp corners and vertical tangents
F: |x|,\ |x-a|\ \text{(corner)};\quad x^{1/3}\ \text{at }0\ \text{(vertical tangent)}
G: y=abs(x-1) | y=cbrt(x) ; -3,3,-2,3

Q: Differentiability of $|f(x)|$, $\max$, $\min$
F: |f(x)|\ \text{not differentiable where } f(x)=0\ \text{and } f'(x)\ne0
N: Count non-differentiable points of $\max/\min$ at the corners where the curves cross.

Q: Functions like $x^n\sin\frac1x$
F: n=1:\ \text{continuous, not differentiable at }0;\quad n=2:\ \text{differentiable at }0,\ f'\ \text{not continuous}
G: y=x*sin(1/x) ; -0.5,0.5,-0.4,0.5

## Mean value theorems
Q: Rolle's theorem
F: f\ \text{continuous on }[a,b],\ \text{differentiable on }(a,b),\ f(a)=f(b)\Rightarrow\exists c:\ f'(c)=0

Q: Lagrange's mean value theorem
F: f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}
N: Geometric: some tangent is parallel to the chord.

Q: Cauchy's mean value theorem
F: \frac{f'(c)}{g'(c)}=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{g(b)-g(a)}

## Common results
Q: Greatest integer at non-integers
F: [x]\ \text{is continuous and differentiable at every non-integer}

Q: Points of discontinuity of $[f(x)]$
F: \text{where } f(x)\ \text{crosses an integer (not just touches from above)}

Q: Functional-equation derivative
F: f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)+2xy,\ f'(0)=k\ \Rightarrow f(x)=x^2+kx

Q: Continuity of composite with sgn
F: \operatorname{sgn}(f(x))\ \text{discontinuous where } f\ \text{changes sign or is } 0 \text{ in isolation}
