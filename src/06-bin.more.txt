## Expansion
Q: $(x-a)^n$ expansion
F: (x-a)^n=\sum_{r=0}^n(-1)^r\,{}^nC_r\,x^{n-r}a^r

Q: Number of terms in $(x+a)^n+(x-a)^n$ and $(x+a)^n-(x-a)^n$
F: n\ \text{even}:\ \tfrac n2+1\ \text{and}\ \tfrac n2;\qquad n\ \text{odd}:\ \tfrac{n+1}2\ \text{each}

Q: $(x+a)^n+(x-a)^n$
F: 2\left[{}^nC_0x^n+{}^nC_2x^{n-2}a^2+\cdots\right]

Q: $r$-th term from the end
F: T_r\ \text{from end}=T_{n-r+2}\ \text{from beginning}

Q: Greatest coefficient
F: n\ \text{even}:\ {}^nC_{n/2};\qquad n\ \text{odd}:\ {}^nC_{(n\pm1)/2}

Q: Coefficient of $x^k$ in $(1+x)^m(1+x)^n$ type products
F: \sum_r(\text{coeff of } x^r\text{ in first})(\text{coeff of } x^{k-r}\text{ in second})

Q: Coefficient of $x^r$ in $(1+x+x^2+\cdots+x^n)$-type products
F: \text{use } \frac{1-x^{n+1}}{1-x}\ \text{and expand } (1-x)^{-k}

Q: Ratio of consecutive coefficients
F: \frac{{}^nC_r}{{}^nC_{r-1}}=\frac{n-r+1}r
N: Three consecutive coefficients in ratio $a:b:c$ → two equations in $n$, $r$.

## Sums of coefficients
Q: $\sum(r+1)C_r$
F: \sum_{r=0}^n(r+1){}^nC_r=(n+2)2^{n-1}

Q: $\sum(-1)^rrC_r$
F: \sum_{r=0}^n(-1)^r\,r\,{}^nC_r=0\quad(n\ge2)

Q: $\sum\frac{C_r}{r+2}$ and $\sum\frac{C_r}{(r+1)(r+2)}$
F: \sum_{r=0}^n\frac{{}^nC_r}{(r+1)(r+2)}=\frac{2^{n+2}-n-3}{(n+1)(n+2)}

Q: $\sum r\,C_r^2$
F: \sum_{r=0}^nr\left({}^nC_r\right)^2=n\,{}^{2n-1}C_{n-1}

Q: $\sum C_{3r}$ (every third coefficient)
F: C_0+C_3+C_6+\cdots=\frac13\left(2^n+2\cos\frac{n\pi}3\right)

Q: Sum $\sum_{r}{}^{r}C_k$ over upper index
F: {}^kC_k+{}^{k+1}C_k+\cdots+{}^nC_k={}^{n+1}C_{k+1}

Q: Sum of coefficients in $(ax+by)^n$
F: (a+b)^n

Q: Product of binomial coefficients
F: C_0C_1\cdots C_n,\qquad\frac{C_1}{C_0}+2\frac{C_2}{C_1}+\cdots+n\frac{C_n}{C_{n-1}}=\frac{n(n+1)}2

## General index
Q: Expansions to remember
F: (1+x)^{-1}=1-x+x^2-\cdots,\quad(1+x)^{-2}=1-2x+3x^2-\cdots,\quad(1-x)^{-3}=\sum\frac{(r+1)(r+2)}2x^r

Q: $\sqrt{1+x}$ and $\frac1{\sqrt{1-4x}}$
F: \sqrt{1+x}=1+\frac x2-\frac{x^2}8+\frac{x^3}{16}-\cdots,\qquad\frac1{\sqrt{1-4x}}=\sum{}^{2r}C_r\,x^r

Q: Coefficient of $x^r$ in $(1-x)^{-n}$
F: {}^{n+r-1}C_r

## Common results
Q: Remainder of $a^n$ divided by $b$
F: \text{write } a^n=(kb\pm1)^m\cdot\ldots,\ \text{expand, keep the last terms}

Q: Comparing large numbers
F: (1.01)^{1000000}\ \text{vs } 10000:\ \text{use } (1+x)^n>1+nx

Q: Number of rational terms in $(a^{1/p}+b^{1/q})^n$
F: \text{count } r\ (0\le r\le n)\ \text{with } p\mid(n-r)\ \text{and } q\mid r

Q: Last two digits of $3^{400}$ type
F: 3^{400}=(10-1)^{200}\equiv1\pmod{100}
