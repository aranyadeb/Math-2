## Basic areas
Q: Area when the curve crosses the axis
F: A=\int_a^c f\,dx-\int_c^b f\,dx\quad(f\ge0 \text{ on } [a,c],\ f\le0 \text{ on } [c,b])

Q: Area in polar form (for reference)
F: A=\frac12\int_{\theta_1}^{\theta_2}r^2\,d\theta

Q: Area enclosed by an inverse function
F: \int_c^df^{-1}(y)\,dy=bd-ac-\int_a^bf(x)\,dx\quad(c=f(a),\ d=f(b))

Q: Area using symmetry
F: \text{compute one symmetric part and multiply}

## Standard areas
Q: Area between $y^2=4ax$ and $x^2=4ay$ (same $a$)
F: \frac{16a^2}3

Q: Area between $y=x^2$ and $y=x$ / $y=x^3$ and $y=x$
F: \frac16;\qquad\frac12\ (\text{both loops})
G: y=x**2 | y=x | y=x**3 ; -1.4,1.6,-1.2,1.6

Q: Area between $y=\sqrt x$ and $y=x^2$
F: \frac13

Q: Area of ellipse segment / quadrant
F: \text{quadrant}=\frac{\pi ab}4;\quad\text{area between ellipse and chord joining ends of axes}=\frac{(\pi-2)ab}4

Q: Area between circle $x^2+y^2=a^2$ and the line $x=\frac a{\sqrt2}$ (minor segment)
F: \frac{a^2}2\left(\frac\pi2-1\right)

Q: Area of a circular segment
F: \frac{r^2}2(\theta-\sin\theta)\quad(\theta=\text{central angle})

Q: Area under $y=e^x$ / $y=\ln x$
F: \int_0^1e^x\,dx=e-1;\qquad\int_1^e\ln x\,dx=1

Q: Area between $y=\sin x$ and $y=\cos x$ on $\left[\frac\pi4,\frac{5\pi}4\right]$
F: 2\sqrt2
G: y=sin(x) | y=cos(x) ; -0.5,6.8,-1.3,1.3

Q: Area bounded by $y=|x|$ and $y=a-x^2$ type
F: \text{find intersections } x=\pm k,\ A=2\int_0^k(a-x^2-x)\,dx

Q: Area between parabola $y^2=4ax$ and a focal chord
F: \frac{8a^2}{3}\cdot\frac{(1+m^2)^{3/2}}{m^3}\ (\text{slope } m)

## Common results
Q: Area bounded by $y=[x]$ or $\{x\}$
F: \int_0^n\{x\}dx=\frac n2;\qquad\text{use rectangles for } [x]

Q: Area bounded by $|x|+|y|\le a$, $|x|\le a$, etc.
F: |x|+|y|\le a:\ 2a^2;\qquad\max(|x|,|y|)\le a:\ 4a^2

Q: Area of region $\{(x,y):y^2\le4x,\ x^2+y^2\le r^2\}$ type
F: \text{split at the intersection; parabola part + circle part}

Q: Ratio in which a curve divides an area
F: \text{compute each part separately and divide}

Q: Area between a curve and its tangent
F: \int(f-\text{tangent})\,dx\ \text{between the contact point and the next intersection}
