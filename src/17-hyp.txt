## Standard hyperbola
Q: Standard hyperbola
F: \frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1,\quad b^2=a^2(e^2-1)
G: p=1.5*cosh(t),1*sinh(t),-2,2 | p=-1.5*cosh(t),1*sinh(t),-2,2 | y=x/1.5 | y=-x/1.5 ; -5,5,-3.5,3.5
N: Foci $(\pm ae,0)$, directrices $x=\pm\frac ae$, latus rectum $\frac{2b^2}a$, transverse axis $2a$, conjugate axis $2b$.

Q: Focal distance property
F: |SP-S'P|=2a;\qquad SP=ex_1-a,\ S'P=ex_1+a\ (\text{right branch})

Q: Conjugate hyperbola
F: -\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1;\qquad\frac1{e_1^2}+\frac1{e_2^2}=1

Q: Parametric point
F: (a\sec\theta,\ b\tan\theta)\quad\text{or}\quad(a\cosh t,\ b\sinh t)

Q: Asymptotes
F: \frac xa\pm\frac yb=0\ \ \Rightarrow\ y=\pm\frac bax
N: Angle between asymptotes $=2\tan^{-1}\frac ba$. Equation of the hyperbola and of its asymptotes differ only by a constant: $H=0$, $A=H+k$, conjugate $=2A-H$.

## Tangents and normals
Q: Tangent at $(x_1,y_1)$ and at $\theta$
F: \frac{xx_1}{a^2}-\frac{yy_1}{b^2}=1;\qquad\frac{x\sec\theta}a-\frac{y\tan\theta}b=1

Q: Slope form and tangency condition
F: y=mx\pm\sqrt{a^2m^2-b^2};\qquad c^2=a^2m^2-b^2
N: Requires $|m|>\frac ba$.

Q: Normal at $(x_1,y_1)$ and at $\theta$
F: \frac{a^2x}{x_1}+\frac{b^2y}{y_1}=a^2+b^2;\qquad ax\cos\theta+by\cot\theta=a^2+b^2

Q: Director circle
F: x^2+y^2=a^2-b^2
N: Exists only if $a>b$.

Q: Chord of contact, midpoint chord, pair of tangents
F: T=0,\qquad T=S_1,\qquad SS_1=T^2

## Rectangular hyperbola
Q: Rectangular hyperbola
F: x^2-y^2=a^2,\ e=\sqrt2;\qquad xy=c^2
G: y=1/x ; -5,5,-5,5
N: Asymptotes of $xy=c^2$ are the axes; vertices $(\pm c,\pm c)$; foci $(\pm\sqrt2c,\pm\sqrt2c)$.

Q: Parametric point and tangent of $xy=c^2$
F: \left(ct,\frac ct\right);\qquad\frac xt+yt=2c
N: Tangent at $(x_1,y_1)$: $xy_1+yx_1=2c^2$.

Q: Normal of $xy=c^2$ at $t$
F: xt^3-yt-ct^4+c=0

Q: Chord joining $t_1,t_2$ on $xy=c^2$
F: x+t_1t_2y=c(t_1+t_2)

## Common results
Q: Product of perpendiculars from foci to a tangent
F: p_1p_2=b^2

Q: Area of triangle formed by a tangent and the asymptotes
F: ab\ (\text{constant})
N: The point of contact is the midpoint of the intercept between the asymptotes.

Q: Product of distances from a point to the asymptotes
F: \frac{a^2b^2}{a^2+b^2}

Q: Reflection property
F: \text{tangent bisects the angle between focal radii (internally)}

Q: Triangle inscribed in $xy=c^2$
F: \text{orthocentre also lies on the hyperbola}

Q: A circle meeting $xy=c^2$ at four points $t_1,\dots,t_4$
F: t_1t_2t_3t_4=1
N: Also the centroid of the four points is the midpoint of the circle's centre and the hyperbola's centre.
