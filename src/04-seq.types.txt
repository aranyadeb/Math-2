## If this type comes, solve like this
Q: If AP data is given (terms, sums) and another term/sum is asked
F: a_n=a+(n-1)d,\quad S_n=\frac n2[2a+(n-1)d],\quad a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}
N: Turn every condition into equations in $a,d$ (2 unknowns ⇒ 2 conditions). If $S_n=An^2+Bn$: $d=2A$, $a=A+B$. Odd/even-term sums: odd terms form an AP with difference $2d$.
P: 13:9,10,11,12,20,21,24,26,27,28,29,39,42,47,52,54,55,57,58,65,67,91,127,129,150,152,4,111

Q: If GP data is given
F: a_n=ar^{n-1},\quad S_n=\frac{a(r^n-1)}{r-1},\quad S_\infty=\frac a{1-r}
N: Divide two conditions to get $r$ ($\frac{a_3+a_4}{a_1+a_2}=r^2$). Products of terms equidistant from ends are equal. Squares of a GP form a GP with ratio $r^2$.
P: 13:3,7,13,14,15,35,40,68,70,72,73,76,131,132,14,18

Q: If terms are in AP and also related by GP (mixed conditions)
F: b^2=ac\ (\text{GP}),\quad2b=a+c\ (\text{AP}),\quad b=\frac{2ac}{a+c}\ (\text{HP})
N: Take convenient terms ($a-d,a,a+d$ or $\frac ar,a,ar$), substitute the second condition.
P: 13:6,16,36,48,50,51,53,56,61,63,71,74,75,133,135,137,86,138,139,79,87,134

Q: If the series is AGP ($\sum n x^n$, $\frac{n^2}{7^n}$, $\sum r\cdot3^r$)
F: S-rS\ \text{repeatedly};\quad\sum_{n\ge1}nx^{n-1}=\frac1{(1-x)^2}
N: For quadratic numerators ($2,6,12,20,\ldots$ over $7^n$) do $S-rS$ twice. Finite: $\sum_{k=1}^nk\,r^{k-1}=\frac{1-(n+1)r^n+nr^{n+1}}{(1-r)^2}$.
P: 13:2,37,41,46,82,83,84,81,22,98

Q: If the $n$-th term must be found from differences (5, 11, 19, 29, …)
F: \text{first differences AP}\Rightarrow T_n=an^2+bn+c;\quad\text{differences GP}\Rightarrow T_n=ar^n+bn+c
N: Fit with $T_1,T_2,T_3$, then sum with $\sum n^2$, $\sum n$.
P: 13:17,108,95,142,156,149,146,145,105,112,107,155

Q: If the term is $\frac{n}{n^4+n^2+1}$ or $\frac{4n}{4+3n^2+n^4}$ or $\frac{n}{1+4n^4}$
F: \frac{n}{n^4+n^2+1}=\frac12\left(\frac1{n^2-n+1}-\frac1{n^2+n+1}\right)
N: Factor $n^4+n^2+1=(n^2+n+1)(n^2-n+1)$; $4n^4+1=(2n^2+2n+1)(2n^2-2n+1)$; telescope.
P: 13:31,44,45,94,96,106,154

Q: If the term is a product reciprocal $\frac1{n(n+1)(n+2)}$ or $\frac1{(a+(n-1)d)(a+nd)}$
F: \frac1{r(r+1)(r+2)}=\frac12\left(\frac1{r(r+1)}-\frac1{(r+1)(r+2)}\right)
P: 13:102,104,119,153,125,30,97,67,122,101

Q: If sums of powers / $\sum S_k$ are asked
F: \sum n=\frac{n(n+1)}2,\ \sum n^2=\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}6,\ \sum n^3=\left[\frac{n(n+1)}2\right]^2
N: Write $T_n$ as a polynomial in $n$ and sum term by term. Ratios like $\frac{\sum k(k+1)^2}{\sum k^2(k+1)}$: compute each.
P: 13:5,8,12,19,38,148,109,115,103,126
