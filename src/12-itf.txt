## Domains, ranges, graphs
Q: Domain and principal range of $\sin^{-1}x$
F: \sin^{-1}:[-1,1]\to\left[-\tfrac\pi2,\tfrac\pi2\right]
G: y=asin(x) ; -1.6,1.6,-1.8,1.8

Q: Domain and principal range of $\cos^{-1}x$
F: \cos^{-1}:[-1,1]\to[0,\pi]
G: y=acos(x) ; -1.6,1.6,-0.3,3.4

Q: Domain and principal range of $\tan^{-1}x$
F: \tan^{-1}:\mathbb R\to\left(-\tfrac\pi2,\tfrac\pi2\right)
G: y=atan(x) ; -6,6,-1.8,1.8

Q: Ranges of $\cot^{-1}$, $\sec^{-1}$, $\csc^{-1}$
F: \cot^{-1}:\mathbb R\to(0,\pi),\quad\sec^{-1}:|x|\ge1\to[0,\pi]-\{\tfrac\pi2\},\quad\csc^{-1}:|x|\ge1\to[-\tfrac\pi2,\tfrac\pi2]-\{0\}
G: y=pi/2-atan(x) ; -6,6,-0.3,3.4

## Properties
Q: Composition identities
F: \sin(\sin^{-1}x)=x\ (|x|\le1);\qquad\sin^{-1}(\sin x)=x\ \left(|x|\le\tfrac\pi2\right)

Q: Graph of $\sin^{-1}(\sin x)$
F: \sin^{-1}(\sin x)=\begin{cases}x,&-\frac\pi2\le x\le\frac\pi2\\\pi-x,&\frac\pi2\le x\le\frac{3\pi}2\\x-2\pi,&\frac{3\pi}2\le x\le\frac{5\pi}2\end{cases}
N: Saw-tooth, period $2\pi$. $\cos^{-1}(\cos x)$ is a triangular wave between $0$ and $\pi$; $\tan^{-1}(\tan x)=x-n\pi$.
G: y=asin(sin(x)) | y=acos(cos(x)) ; -7,7,-2,3.5

Q: Negative arguments
F: \sin^{-1}(-x)=-\sin^{-1}x,\quad\tan^{-1}(-x)=-\tan^{-1}x,\quad\cos^{-1}(-x)=\pi-\cos^{-1}x,\quad\cot^{-1}(-x)=\pi-\cot^{-1}x

Q: Reciprocal arguments
F: \sin^{-1}\tfrac1x=\csc^{-1}x,\qquad\tan^{-1}\tfrac1x=\begin{cases}\cot^{-1}x,&x>0\\-\pi+\cot^{-1}x,&x<0\end{cases}

Q: Complementary sums
F: \sin^{-1}x+\cos^{-1}x=\frac\pi2,\quad\tan^{-1}x+\cot^{-1}x=\frac\pi2,\quad\sec^{-1}x+\csc^{-1}x=\frac\pi2

## Sum and difference
Q: Sum of inverse tangents
F: \tan^{-1}x+\tan^{-1}y=\begin{cases}\tan^{-1}\frac{x+y}{1-xy},&xy<1\\\pi+\tan^{-1}\frac{x+y}{1-xy},&x,y>0,\ xy>1\\-\pi+\tan^{-1}\frac{x+y}{1-xy},&x,y<0,\ xy>1\end{cases}
N: $xy=1$, $x>0$: sum $=\frac\pi2$.

Q: Difference of inverse tangents
F: \tan^{-1}x-\tan^{-1}y=\tan^{-1}\frac{x-y}{1+xy}\quad(xy>-1)

Q: Sum of inverse sines
F: \sin^{-1}x+\sin^{-1}y=\sin^{-1}\left(x\sqrt{1-y^2}+y\sqrt{1-x^2}\right)
N: Valid when $x,y\ge0$ and $x^2+y^2\le1$ (other cases add/subtract $\pi$).

Q: Sum of inverse cosines
F: \cos^{-1}x+\cos^{-1}y=\cos^{-1}\left(xy-\sqrt{1-x^2}\sqrt{1-y^2}\right)\quad(x+y\ge0)

Q: Three inverse tangents
F: \tan^{-1}x+\tan^{-1}y+\tan^{-1}z=\tan^{-1}\frac{x+y+z-xyz}{1-xy-yz-zx}

## Multiple angles
Q: $2\tan^{-1}x$ forms
F: 2\tan^{-1}x=\sin^{-1}\frac{2x}{1+x^2}\ (|x|\le1)=\cos^{-1}\frac{1-x^2}{1+x^2}\ (x\ge0)=\tan^{-1}\frac{2x}{1-x^2}\ (|x|<1)

Q: $2\sin^{-1}x$ and $2\cos^{-1}x$
F: 2\sin^{-1}x=\sin^{-1}\left(2x\sqrt{1-x^2}\right)\ \left(|x|\le\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\right),\qquad2\cos^{-1}x=\cos^{-1}(2x^2-1)\ (0\le x\le1)

Q: Triple forms
F: 3\sin^{-1}x=\sin^{-1}(3x-4x^3),\quad3\cos^{-1}x=\cos^{-1}(4x^3-3x),\quad3\tan^{-1}x=\tan^{-1}\frac{3x-x^3}{1-3x^2}
N: Valid on the restricted intervals $|x|\le\frac12$ (for sin, tan: $|x|<\frac1{\sqrt3}$), $\frac12\le x\le1$ for cos.

## Conversions
Q: Converting between inverse functions
F: \sin^{-1}x=\cos^{-1}\sqrt{1-x^2}=\tan^{-1}\frac x{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\quad(0\le x<1)
H: Draw a right triangle with opposite $x$, hypotenuse $1$.

## Common results
Q: Telescoping inverse tangent sums
F: \tan^{-1}\frac{1}{1+r(r+1)}=\tan^{-1}(r+1)-\tan^{-1}r
N: General trick: write $\frac{x-y}{1+xy}$ form. $\sum_{r=1}^\infty\tan^{-1}\frac1{2r^2}=\frac\pi4$.

Q: Useful values
F: \tan^{-1}1+\tan^{-1}2+\tan^{-1}3=\pi,\qquad\tan^{-1}\tfrac12+\tan^{-1}\tfrac13=\tfrac\pi4

Q: Sum of $\sin^{-1}$ constraint
F: \sin^{-1}x+\sin^{-1}y+\sin^{-1}z=\frac{3\pi}2\Rightarrow x=y=z=1
N: Similar extreme-value tricks: $\cos^{-1}x+\cos^{-1}y+\cos^{-1}z=3\pi\Rightarrow x=y=z=-1$.
