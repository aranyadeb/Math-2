## Basic areas
Q: Area between a curve and the x-axis
F: A=\int_a^b|f(x)|\,dx

Q: Area between a curve and the y-axis
F: A=\int_c^d|g(y)|\,dy

Q: Area between two curves
F: A=\int_a^b\left[f(x)-g(x)\right]dx\quad(f\ge g)
N: Find the intersection points first; use horizontal strips when the curves are easier as $x=g(y)$.

Q: Parametric curve
F: A=\left|\int_{t_1}^{t_2}y\,\frac{dx}{dt}\,dt\right|

## Standard areas
Q: Area between $y^2=4ax$ and $x^2=4by$
F: A=\frac{16ab}3
G: y=sqrt(4*x) | y=x**2/4 ; -0.5,5,-0.5,5

Q: Area between $y^2=4ax$ and its latus rectum
F: A=\frac83a^2

Q: Area between $y^2=4ax$ and $y=mx$
F: A=\frac{8a^2}{3m^3}

Q: Area of a circle and ellipse
F: \pi r^2,\qquad\pi ab

Q: Area under one arch of $\sin x$
F: \int_0^\pi\sin x\,dx=2
G: y=sin(x) ; -0.5,7,-1.3,1.3

Q: Area bounded by $|x|+|y|=a$
F: 2a^2
G: y=1-abs(x) | y=abs(x)-1 ; -1.6,1.6,-1.3,1.3

Q: Area of the region $|x-a|+|y-b|\le c$
F: 2c^2

Q: Area of the astroid $x^{2/3}+y^{2/3}=a^{2/3}$
F: \frac{3\pi a^2}8
G: p=cos(t)**3,sin(t)**3,0,2*pi ; -1.4,1.4,-1.2,1.2

Q: Area of the loop / curve tracing tips
F: \text{check symmetry, intercepts, asymptotes, and sign of } y\ \text{before integrating}

## Common results
Q: Parabola segment (Archimedes)
F: \text{area of a parabolic segment}=\frac23\times\text{area of the enclosing rectangle}
N: Area between $y=x^2$ and $y=c$: $\frac43c^{3/2}$.

Q: Area between a parabola and a chord in terms of parameters
F: \frac{a^2}{3}|t_1-t_2|^3\quad(y^2=4ax)

Q: Area between $y=f(x)$ and its inverse
F: A=2\times(\text{area between } y=f(x) \text{ and } y=x)
N: Valid when $f$ is increasing, since the two curves are mirror images in $y=x$.
