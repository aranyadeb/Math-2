## Arithmetic progression
Q: Properties of an AP
F: a_p=q,\ a_q=p\Rightarrow a_{p+q}=0;\qquad S_p=q,\ S_q=p\Rightarrow S_{p+q}=-(p+q)
N: $S_p=S_q\Rightarrow S_{p+q}=0$. $p\,a_p=q\,a_q\Rightarrow a_{p+q}=0$.

Q: Sum of terms equidistant from the ends
F: a_k+a_{n-k+1}=a_1+a_n

Q: Middle term of an AP
F: n\ \text{odd}:\ S_n=n\times\text{middle term}

Q: Sum of $m$ terms of $\frac{S_m}{S_n}$ ratio problems
F: \frac{S_m}{S'_m}=\frac{Am+B}{Cm+D}\Rightarrow\frac{a_n}{a'_n}=\frac{A(2n-1)+B}{C(2n-1)+D}

Q: Common difference from sums
F: d=S_n-2S_{n-1}+S_{n-2}

## Geometric progression
Q: Product of $n$ terms of a GP
F: P_n=a^nr^{\frac{n(n-1)}2}=(a\,l)^{n/2}

Q: Properties of a GP
F: a_ka_{n-k+1}=a_1a_n;\qquad\text{if all } a_i>0,\ \log a_i\ \text{form an AP}

Q: Infinite GP with $|r|<1$ sum of squares
F: a^2+a^2r^2+\cdots=\frac{a^2}{1-r^2}

Q: Sum of GP with alternating sign
F: a-ar+ar^2-\cdots=\frac a{1+r}

## Harmonic progression
Q: HP from AP terms
F: a,b,c\ \text{in HP}\iff\frac1a,\frac1b,\frac1c\ \text{in AP}\iff b=\frac{2ac}{a+c}

Q: n HMs between $a$ and $b$
F: \frac1{H_k}=\frac1a+k\cdot\frac{a-b}{(n+1)ab}

Q: A, G, H for $n$ numbers relation
F: A\ge G\ge H;\ \text{for two numbers } A,G,H \text{ are in GP}

Q: Mixed progressions
F: a,b,c\ \text{AP and GP}\Rightarrow a=b=c;\qquad\text{AP and HP}\Rightarrow a=b=c

## AGP and special series
Q: $\sum r\,x^{r-1}$ (differentiated GP)
F: \sum_{r=1}^{\infty}rx^{r-1}=\frac1{(1-x)^2},\quad|x|<1

Q: $\sum_{r=1}^n r\cdot2^r$
F: (n-1)2^{n+1}+2

Q: $\sum r(r+1)$ and $\sum r(r+1)(r+2)(r+3)$
F: \sum_{r=1}^nr(r+1)=\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}3,\qquad\sum r(r+1)(r+2)(r+3)=\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)(n+4)}5

Q: Sum of products of consecutive reciprocals (general)
F: \sum_{r=1}^n\frac1{(a+(r-1)d)(a+rd)}=\frac n{a(a+nd)}

Q: Sum of $n$-th term $T_n=\frac1{n(n+1)}\cdot\ldots$ via partial fractions
F: \frac1{r(r+k)}=\frac1k\left(\frac1r-\frac1{r+k}\right)

Q: Series of the form $\sum\frac{r}{r^4+r^2+1}$
F: \frac r{r^4+r^2+1}=\frac12\left(\frac1{r^2-r+1}-\frac1{r^2+r+1}\right),\qquad\sum_{r=1}^n=\frac12\left(1-\frac1{n^2+n+1}\right)

Q: Second differences constant
F: T_n=an^2+bn+c\ (\text{solve from first three terms})

Q: Sum of the series $1+11+111+\cdots$
F: \frac{10^{n+1}-10-9n}{81}
N: $0.1+0.11+\cdots$ ($n$ terms) $=\frac{n}{9}-\frac{1}{81}\left(1-10^{-n}\right)$.

Q: Sum of the first $n$ terms of $\sum_{r}\frac1{\sqrt r+\sqrt{r+1}}$
F: \sqrt{n+1}-1

## Common results
Q: Sum of first $n$ even numbers and their squares/cubes
F: \sum2k=n(n+1),\qquad\sum(2k)^2=\frac{2n(n+1)(2n+1)}3,\qquad\sum(2k-1)^3=n^2(2n^2-1)

Q: Sum of $k$ terms from both ends
F: \sum_{k=1}^n(n-k+1)k=\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}6

Q: Number of terms in an AP between two given numbers
F: n=\frac{l-a}d+1

Q: Arithmetico-geometric with $r=x$ and difference 1
F: 1+2x+3x^2+\cdots+nx^{n-1}=\frac{1-(n+1)x^n+nx^{n+1}}{(1-x)^2}

Q: Harmonic series bounds
F: \ln(n+1)<1+\frac12+\cdots+\frac1n<1+\ln n
