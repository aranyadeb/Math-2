Q: If an equation in $z$ and $\bar z$ is given (find solutions / $\sum|z|^2$)
F: z=x+iy:\ \text{equate real and imaginary parts}
N: Or take modulus of both sides first ($|\bar z|=|z|$) to get $|z|$, then argument.

Q: If powers of $\omega$, $1\pm i$, $-1+i\sqrt3$ appear
F: -1+i\sqrt3=2\omega,\ \ 1+i=\sqrt2e^{i\pi/4},\ \ \omega^3=1,\ 1+\omega+\omega^2=0
N: Convert to polar form, apply De Moivre, reduce angles mod $2\pi$.
P: 26:4,5,11,14,20,25,40,41,43,44,67,72,39,3

Q: If a locus is described ($|z-a|=k|z-b|$, $\arg\frac{z-a}{z-b}=\theta$, $\operatorname{Re}/\operatorname{Im}$ conditions)
F: |z-a|=|z-b|:\ \text{perpendicular bisector};\ \ \ne1\ \text{ratio: circle};\ \ \arg=\theta:\ \text{arc}
N: Write $z=x+iy$ and simplify to a Cartesian circle/line when unsure.
