## Expansion
Q: Binomial theorem
F: (x+a)^n=\sum_{r=0}^{n}{}^nC_r\,x^{n-r}a^r
N: $n+1$ terms, $n\in\mathbb N$.
D: Induction: assume for $n$, multiply by $(x+a)$, and use ${}^nC_r+{}^nC_{r-1}={}^{n+1}C_r$.

Q: General term
F: T_{r+1}={}^nC_r\,x^{n-r}a^r

Q: Middle term(s)
F: n\ \text{even}:\ T_{\frac n2+1};\qquad n\ \text{odd}:\ T_{\frac{n+1}2},\ T_{\frac{n+3}2}

Q: Term independent of $x$ in $\left(x^\alpha+\frac1{x^\beta}\right)^n$
F: \alpha(n-r)-\beta r=0\ \Rightarrow\ r=\frac{\alpha n}{\alpha+\beta}

Q: Numerically greatest term in $(1+x)^n$
F: \frac{T_{r+1}}{T_r}=\frac{n-r+1}{r}|x|\ge1\iff r\le\frac{(n+1)|x|}{1+|x|}
N: If $m=\frac{(n+1)|x|}{1+|x|}$ is an integer, $T_m$ and $T_{m+1}$ are equal and greatest; else $T_{[m]+1}$.

Q: Number of terms in a multinomial expansion
F: (x_1+\cdots+x_r)^n:\ {}^{n+r-1}C_{r-1}\ \text{terms}

Q: Multinomial coefficient
F: (x_1+\cdots+x_k)^n=\sum\frac{n!}{n_1!\cdots n_k!}x_1^{n_1}\cdots x_k^{n_k},\quad\sum n_i=n

## Sums of coefficients
Q: Sum of all binomial coefficients
F: C_0+C_1+\cdots+C_n=2^n
D: Put $x=1$ in $(1+x)^n=\sum C_rx^r$.

Q: Alternate sums
F: C_0+C_2+C_4+\cdots=C_1+C_3+\cdots=2^{n-1}
D: Put $x=-1$: $\sum(-1)^rC_r=0$; combine with the total $2^n$.

Q: Weighted sum $\sum rC_r$
F: \sum_{r=0}^n r\,C_r=n2^{n-1},\qquad\sum r^2C_r=n(n+1)2^{n-2}
D: Differentiate $(1+x)^n$ and put $x=1$.

Q: Sum $\sum\frac{C_r}{r+1}$
F: \sum_{r=0}^n\frac{C_r}{r+1}=\frac{2^{n+1}-1}{n+1}
D: Integrate $(1+x)^n$ from $0$ to $1$.

Q: Alternating version
F: \sum_{r=0}^n\frac{(-1)^rC_r}{r+1}=\frac1{n+1}

Q: Vandermonde's identity
F: \sum_{r}{}^mC_r\,{}^nC_{k-r}={}^{m+n}C_k
N: Special: $C_0^2+C_1^2+\cdots+C_n^2={}^{2n}C_n$.
D: Compare coefficient of $x^k$ in $(1+x)^m(1+x)^n=(1+x)^{m+n}$.

Q: Product sum $\sum C_rC_{r+k}$
F: C_0C_k+C_1C_{k+1}+\cdots+C_{n-k}C_n={}^{2n}C_{n-k}

Q: Alternating squares
F: C_0^2-C_1^2+C_2^2-\cdots=\begin{cases}0,&n\ \text{odd}\\(-1)^{n/2}\,{}^nC_{n/2},&n\ \text{even}\end{cases}

Q: Sum of coefficients of a polynomial
F: f(1);\qquad\text{even-power coefficients}=\frac{f(1)+f(-1)}2

## General index
Q: Binomial theorem for any index
F: (1+x)^n=1+nx+\frac{n(n-1)}{2!}x^2+\cdots,\quad|x|<1
N: General term $\frac{n(n-1)\cdots(n-r+1)}{r!}x^r$.

Q: Standard expansions
F: (1-x)^{-1}=\sum x^r,\quad(1-x)^{-2}=\sum(r+1)x^r,\quad(1-x)^{-n}=\sum{}^{n+r-1}C_r\,x^r

Q: Approximation for small $x$
F: (1+x)^n\approx1+nx

## Common results
Q: Divisibility / remainder trick
F: (1+a)^n=1+na+\binom n2a^2+\cdots\ \Rightarrow\ (1+a)^n\equiv1+na\pmod{a^2}
N: E.g. $7^{2n}=49^n=(1+48)^n$.

Q: Integral and fractional part trick
F: (\sqrt a+b)^n=I+f,\ \ (\sqrt a-b)^n=f'\ (0<f'<1):\ \ I+f\pm f'\ \text{is an integer}
N: If $n$ is odd and $\sqrt a-b>0$, $f=f'$; then $(I+f)f=(a-b^2)^n$.

Q: Last digits
F: \text{use } (10k\pm1)^n\ \text{expansion modulo } 10, 100
