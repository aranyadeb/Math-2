## Tangents and normals
Q: Tangent and normal at $(x_1,y_1)$
F: y-y_1=m(x-x_1),\qquad y-y_1=-\frac1m(x-x_1),\quad m=\left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{(x_1,y_1)}

Q: Lengths of tangent, normal, subtangent, subnormal
F: \text{subtangent}=\left|\frac y{y'}\right|,\ \text{subnormal}=|yy'|,\ \text{tangent}=\left|\frac{y\sqrt{1+y'^2}}{y'}\right|,\ \text{normal}=|y\sqrt{1+y'^2}|

Q: Angle between two curves
F: \tan\theta=\left|\frac{m_1-m_2}{1+m_1m_2}\right|
N: Orthogonal curves: $m_1m_2=-1$ at the intersection.

Q: Rate of change
F: \frac{dy}{dt}=\frac{dy}{dx}\cdot\frac{dx}{dt}
N: Approximation: $\Delta y\approx f'(x)\Delta x$; relative error $\frac{\Delta y}y$.

## Monotonicity
Q: Increasing and decreasing
F: f'(x)>0\Rightarrow\text{strictly increasing};\quad f'(x)<0\Rightarrow\text{strictly decreasing}
N: $f'\ge0$ with equality only at isolated points still gives strictly increasing.

Q: Using monotonicity for inequalities
F: g(x)=f_1(x)-f_2(x),\ g(a)=0,\ g'>0\ \text{on }(a,b)\Rightarrow f_1>f_2\ \text{on }(a,b)
N: E.g. $\ln(1+x)<x$, $e^x\ge1+x$, $\frac{\sin x}x$ decreasing on $(0,\frac\pi2)$.

## Maxima and minima
Q: First derivative test
F: f'\ \text{changes } +\to-:\ \text{local max};\quad -\to+:\ \text{local min}

Q: Second derivative test
F: f'(c)=0:\ f''(c)<0\Rightarrow\text{max},\quad f''(c)>0\Rightarrow\text{min}
N: If $f''(c)=0$: check the first non-zero higher derivative; odd order → no extremum (point of inflection), even order → extremum.

Q: Absolute extrema on $[a,b]$
F: \text{compare } f\ \text{at critical points and at } a,\ b

Q: Point of inflection
F: f''(x)\ \text{changes sign};\ \ \text{concave up if } f''>0,\ \text{down if } f''<0

## Common results
Q: Standard optimisation results
F: \text{fixed perimeter}\Rightarrow\text{square has max area};\quad\text{fixed area}\Rightarrow\text{square has min perimeter}
N: Max rectangle in a circle of radius $r$: square, area $2r^2$. Max rectangle in an ellipse: $2ab$.

Q: Cylinder and cone results
F: \text{open cylinder, fixed volume, min surface}: h=r;\quad\text{closed cylinder}: h=2r
N: Cone of max volume in a sphere of radius $R$: $h=\frac{4R}3$. Cylinder of max volume in a sphere: $h=\frac{2R}{\sqrt3}$. Cone with fixed slant height, max volume: $\tan\theta=\sqrt2$ (semi-vertical angle).

Q: Shortest distance from a point to a curve
F: \text{along the common normal}

Q: Number of roots via graph
F: f(x)=k\ \text{has as many roots as intersections of } y=f(x) \text{ and } y=k
N: Use local max/min values: between them → 3 roots for a cubic.
G: y=x**3-3*x | y=1 ; -3,3,-4,4

Q: Cubic with three real roots
F: x^3+px+q=0\ \text{has three distinct real roots iff } 4p^3+27q^2<0

Q: Global inequality from convexity
F: f''>0:\ f\!\left(\frac{a+b}2\right)\le\frac{f(a)+f(b)}2\ (\text{Jensen})
