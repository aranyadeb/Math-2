## Basics and graphs
Q: Radian–degree conversion
F: \pi\ \text{rad}=180^\circ,\qquad l=r\theta
N: Area of a sector $=\frac12r^2\theta$.

Q: Pythagorean identities
F: \sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1,\quad 1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta,\quad1+\cot^2\theta=\csc^2\theta
N: $\sec\theta+\tan\theta=\frac1{\sec\theta-\tan\theta}$ (useful pair trick).

Q: Signs in quadrants
F: \text{I: all}+,\ \ \text{II: }\sin,\csc+,\ \ \text{III: }\tan,\cot+,\ \ \text{IV: }\cos,\sec+
H: "All Students Take Coffee".

Q: Graph of $\sin x$ and $\cos x$
F: \sin x,\cos x\in[-1,1],\ \text{period }2\pi
G: y=sin(x) | y=cos(x) ; -6.6,6.6,-1.6,1.6

Q: Graph of $\tan x$
F: \tan x\in\mathbb R,\ \text{period }\pi,\ \text{asymptotes } x=(2n+1)\tfrac\pi2
G: y=tan(x) ; -4.8,4.8,-4,4

Q: Graphs of $\csc x$, $\sec x$, $\cot x$
F: \csc x,\sec x\in(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty),\quad\cot x\in\mathbb R
G: y=1/sin(x) | y=1/cos(x) | y=1/tan(x) ; -6.6,6.6,-4,4

Q: Allied angles (reduction)
F: \sin(\pi-\theta)=\sin\theta,\ \cos(\pi-\theta)=-\cos\theta,\ \sin(\tfrac\pi2-\theta)=\cos\theta,\ \sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta
N: Odd multiple of $\frac\pi2$: the function changes to its co-function; even multiple: it stays. Sign from the quadrant of the original angle.

Q: Standard values
F: \sin15^\circ=\frac{\sqrt3-1}{2\sqrt2},\quad\cos15^\circ=\frac{\sqrt3+1}{2\sqrt2},\quad\tan15^\circ=2-\sqrt3,\quad\tan75^\circ=2+\sqrt3

Q: Values for 18°, 36°
F: \sin18^\circ=\frac{\sqrt5-1}4,\qquad\cos36^\circ=\frac{\sqrt5+1}4
N: $\cos18^\circ=\frac{\sqrt{10+2\sqrt5}}4$, $\sin36^\circ=\frac{\sqrt{10-2\sqrt5}}4$.
D: Let $\theta=18^\circ$: $2\theta=90^\circ-3\theta \Rightarrow \sin2\theta=\cos3\theta \Rightarrow 2\sin\theta=4\cos^2\theta-3$ → $4\sin^2\theta+2\sin\theta-1=0$.

Q: Values for 22.5°, 7.5°
F: \tan22.5^\circ=\sqrt2-1,\qquad\tan67.5^\circ=\sqrt2+1
N: $\sin22.5^\circ=\frac{\sqrt{2-\sqrt2}}2$, $\cos22.5^\circ=\frac{\sqrt{2+\sqrt2}}2$.

## Compound angles
Q: Sine of sum/difference
F: \sin(A\pm B)=\sin A\cos B\pm\cos A\sin B

Q: Cosine of sum/difference
F: \cos(A\pm B)=\cos A\cos B\mp\sin A\sin B

Q: Tangent of sum/difference
F: \tan(A\pm B)=\frac{\tan A\pm\tan B}{1\mp\tan A\tan B}
N: $\cot(A\pm B)=\frac{\cot A\cot B\mp1}{\cot B\pm\cot A}$.

Q: Product identities
F: \sin(A+B)\sin(A-B)=\sin^2A-\sin^2B,\qquad\cos(A+B)\cos(A-B)=\cos^2A-\sin^2B

Q: Tangent of three angles
F: \tan(A+B+C)=\frac{\sum\tan A-\prod\tan A}{1-\sum\tan A\tan B}

## Multiple and sub-multiple angles
Q: Double angle
F: \sin2A=2\sin A\cos A=\frac{2\tan A}{1+\tan^2A},\quad\cos2A=\cos^2A-\sin^2A=\frac{1-\tan^2A}{1+\tan^2A}
N: $\cos2A=2\cos^2A-1=1-2\sin^2A$; $\tan2A=\frac{2\tan A}{1-\tan^2A}$.

Q: Triple angle
F: \sin3A=3\sin A-4\sin^3A,\quad\cos3A=4\cos^3A-3\cos A,\quad\tan3A=\frac{3\tan A-\tan^3A}{1-3\tan^2A}

Q: Half-angle (power reduction)
F: \sin^2\tfrac A2=\frac{1-\cos A}2,\quad\cos^2\tfrac A2=\frac{1+\cos A}2,\quad\tan\tfrac A2=\frac{1-\cos A}{\sin A}=\frac{\sin A}{1+\cos A}

Q: Product of sines at 60° spacing
F: \sin\theta\sin(60^\circ-\theta)\sin(60^\circ+\theta)=\tfrac14\sin3\theta
N: Same pattern: $\cos\theta\cos(60^\circ-\theta)\cos(60^\circ+\theta)=\frac14\cos3\theta$, $\tan\theta\tan(60^\circ-\theta)\tan(60^\circ+\theta)=\tan3\theta$.

## Sum–product
Q: Sum to product
F: \sin C+\sin D=2\sin\tfrac{C+D}2\cos\tfrac{C-D}2,\qquad\sin C-\sin D=2\cos\tfrac{C+D}2\sin\tfrac{C-D}2
N: $\cos C+\cos D=2\cos\frac{C+D}2\cos\frac{C-D}2$, $\cos C-\cos D=-2\sin\frac{C+D}2\sin\frac{C-D}2$.

Q: Product to sum
F: 2\sin A\cos B=\sin(A+B)+\sin(A-B),\quad2\cos A\cos B=\cos(A+B)+\cos(A-B)
N: $2\sin A\sin B=\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)$.

## Series and products
Q: Sum of sines in AP
F: \sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\sin(\alpha+k\beta)=\frac{\sin\frac{n\beta}2}{\sin\frac\beta2}\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{(n-1)\beta}2\right)
N: For cosines replace the last $\sin$ with $\cos$. Hook: (sin of n×half-difference / sin of half-difference) × sin of average angle.
D: Multiply by $2\sin\frac\beta2$; each term becomes a difference of cosines that telescopes.

Q: Product of cosines in GP of angles
F: \cos A\cos2A\cos4A\cdots\cos2^{n-1}A=\frac{\sin2^nA}{2^n\sin A}
D: Multiply and divide by $2\sin A$ and use $2\sin x\cos x=\sin2x$ repeatedly.

## Range and maxima
Q: Range of $a\sin\theta+b\cos\theta$
F: -\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\le a\sin\theta+b\cos\theta\le\sqrt{a^2+b^2}
D: Write $a\sin\theta+b\cos\theta=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\sin(\theta+\phi)$ with $\tan\phi=\frac ba$.

Q: Minimum of $a\tan^2\theta+b\cot^2\theta$
F: 2\sqrt{ab}\quad(a,b>0)
N: Similarly $a\sin^2\theta+b\csc^2\theta$ has minimum $2\sqrt{ab}$ only if $a\ge b$, else $a+b$.

## Common results
Q: Conditional identities when $A+B+C=\pi$
F: \tan A+\tan B+\tan C=\tan A\tan B\tan C
N: Also $\cot\frac A2\cot\frac B2\cot\frac C2=\sum\cot\frac A2$ and $\sum\tan\frac A2\tan\frac B2=1$.

Q: Sine/cosine sums in a triangle
F: \sin2A+\sin2B+\sin2C=4\sin A\sin B\sin C,\qquad \cos2A+\cos2B+\cos2C=-1-4\cos A\cos B\cos C
N: $\sin A+\sin B+\sin C=4\cos\frac A2\cos\frac B2\cos\frac C2$; $\cos A+\cos B+\cos C=1+4\sin\frac A2\sin\frac B2\sin\frac C2$.

Q: Squares in a triangle
F: \cos^2A+\cos^2B+\cos^2C=1-2\cos A\cos B\cos C

Q: Useful products
F: \cos20^\circ\cos40^\circ\cos80^\circ=\tfrac18,\quad\sin20^\circ\sin40^\circ\sin80^\circ=\tfrac{\sqrt3}8,\quad\tan20^\circ\tan40^\circ\tan80^\circ=\sqrt3

Q: Useful sums
F: \cos\tfrac{2\pi}7+\cos\tfrac{4\pi}7+\cos\tfrac{6\pi}7=-\tfrac12,\qquad\cos\tfrac{\pi}{7}\cos\tfrac{2\pi}7\cos\tfrac{4\pi}7=-\tfrac18

Q: Bounds
F: \sin\theta<\theta<\tan\theta\quad(0<\theta<\tfrac\pi2)
