## Basics
Q: General vs particular solution
F: \text{general: has } n \text{ arbitrary constants};\quad\text{particular: constants fixed by initial conditions}

Q: DE of all circles / parabolas families (orders)
F: \text{circles in a plane}: 3;\quad\text{circles through origin}: 2;\quad\text{parabolas with axis along } x\text{-axis}: 2

Q: DE of all non-vertical lines
F: \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=0

## First-order methods
Q: Separable after substitution $y=vx$ examples
F: x\,dy-y\,dx=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\,dx\Rightarrow y+\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=cx^2

Q: Equations of the form $\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{a_1x+b_1y+c_1}{k(a_1x+b_1y)+c_2}$
F: \text{put } v=a_1x+b_1y\ \text{(lines parallel)}

Q: Standard integrating factors to remember
F: P=\frac1x\Rightarrow\text{IF}=x;\quad P=-\frac1x\Rightarrow\frac1x;\quad P=\tan x\Rightarrow\sec x;\quad P=\cot x\Rightarrow\sin x
N: $P=\frac{2x}{1+x^2}\Rightarrow 1+x^2$; $P=\frac{1}{x\ln x}\Rightarrow\ln x$.

Q: Equations reducible to linear: $f'(y)\frac{dy}{dx}+Pf(y)=Q$
F: \text{put } t=f(y)
N: E.g. $\sec^2y\frac{dy}{dx}+P\tan y=Q$: $t=\tan y$.

Q: Exact by inspection groupings
F: x\,dx+y\,dy=\tfrac12d(x^2+y^2);\quad\frac{x\,dy-y\,dx}{y^2}... \to -d\left(\tfrac xy\right)\ \text{form};\quad\frac{dx+dy}{x+y}=d\ln(x+y)

Q: Higher-degree first-order equations
F: (y')^2-(x+y)y'+xy=0\Rightarrow(y'-x)(y'-y)=0\ \text{(solve each factor)}

## Applications
Q: Population / interest growth
F: \frac{dP}{dt}=rP\Rightarrow P=P_0e^{rt};\qquad\text{doubling time}=\frac{\ln2}r

Q: Newton's law of cooling
F: T-T_s=(T_0-T_s)e^{-kt}

Q: Curve whose tangent / normal has a property
F: \text{write the property with } y'\ \text{and solve the DE}
N: Subtangent constant $k$: $y=Ce^{x/k}$. Subnormal constant $k$: $y^2=2kx+C$.

Q: Mixing / tank problems
F: \frac{dQ}{dt}=(\text{rate in})-(\text{rate out})

## Common results
Q: Solution of $\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{y}{x}$, $\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac xy$, $\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac xy$
F: y=cx;\qquad x^2+y^2=c;\qquad y^2-x^2=c

Q: Solution of $\frac{dy}{dx}+y=e^{-x}$
F: ye^x=x+C

Q: Solution of $y'=e^{x-y}$ / $y'=e^{x+y}$
F: e^y=e^x+C;\qquad -e^{-y}=e^x+C

Q: Maximum / value of the solution at a point
F: \text{solve fully, apply the initial condition, then evaluate or differentiate}
