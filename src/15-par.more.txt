@ par | Parabola | 11 | Coordinate Geometry

## Standard parabola
Q: Parts of $x^2=4ay$
F: \text{focus }(0,a),\ \text{directrix } y=-a,\ \text{axis } x=0,\ \text{LR}=4a

Q: Parabola with general focus and directrix
F: (x-\alpha)^2+(y-\beta)^2=\frac{(lx+my+n)^2}{l^2+m^2}

Q: Position of a point w.r.t. $y^2=4ax$
F: S_1=y_1^2-4ax_1:\ <0\ \text{inside},\ =0\ \text{on},\ >0\ \text{outside}

Q: Parametric forms for other standard parabolas
F: x^2=4ay:\ (2at,\ at^2);\qquad y^2=-4ax:\ (-at^2,\ 2at)

Q: Vertex form $y=a(x-h)^2+k$
F: \text{vertex }(h,k),\ \text{focus }\left(h,\ k+\tfrac1{4a}\right),\ \text{directrix } y=k-\tfrac1{4a}

## Chords
Q: Length of the chord joining $t_1,t_2$
F: a|t_1-t_2|\sqrt{(t_1+t_2)^2+4}

Q: Chord subtending a right angle at the vertex
F: t_1t_2=-4;\ \text{such chords pass through } (4a,0)

Q: Chord through a fixed point $(k,0)$ on the axis
F: t_1t_2=-\frac ka

Q: Chord of contact and pair of tangents
F: yy_1=2a(x+x_1);\qquad(y^2-4ax)(y_1^2-4ax_1)=(yy_1-2a(x+x_1))^2

Q: Locus of midpoints of focal chords
F: y^2=2a(x-a)

## Tangents
Q: Tangent to $x^2=4ay$
F: x=my+\frac am\ \ \text{or}\ \ y=mx-am^2

Q: Angle between tangents at $t_1$, $t_2$
F: \tan\theta=\left|\frac{t_1-t_2}{1+t_1t_2}\right|

Q: Locus of intersection of tangents meeting at angle $\theta$
F: y^2-4ax=(x+a)^2\tan^2\theta

Q: Tangent intercepts
F: \text{tangent at } P \text{ meets the axis at } (-x_1,0);\ SP=ST\ (\text{focus equidistant})

Q: Common tangent of $y^2=4ax$ and $x^2=4by$
F: a^{1/3}x+b^{1/3}y+a^{2/3}b^{2/3}=0

Q: Common tangent of $y^2=4ax$ and $x^2+y^2=r^2$
F: y=mx+\frac am,\ \ \frac{a^2}{m^2}=r^2(1+m^2)

## Normals
Q: Normal at $(x_1,y_1)$
F: y-y_1=-\frac{y_1}{2a}(x-x_1)

Q: Normal to $x^2=4ay$ in slope form
F: y=mx+2a+\frac a{m^2}

Q: Normal chord length
F: \text{normal at } t:\ \frac{4a(1+t^2)^{3/2}}{t^2}

Q: Minimum length of normal chord
F: 6\sqrt3\,a\quad(t^2=2)

Q: Feet of normals from $(h,k)$
F: am^3+(2a-h)m+k=0;\quad\text{sum of slopes } 0,\ \ \sum m_1m_2=\frac{2a-h}a,\ \ m_1m_2m_3=-\frac ka

## Common results
Q: Circle on a focal chord as diameter
F: \text{touches the directrix}

Q: Circle on focal distance as diameter
F: \text{touches the tangent at the vertex}

Q: Reflected rays
F: \text{rays parallel to the axis reflect through the focus}

Q: Circle through the vertices of a triangle formed by three tangents
F: \text{circumcircle of the triangle formed by three tangents passes through the focus}

Q: Orthocentre of triangle formed by three tangents
F: \text{lies on the directrix}
