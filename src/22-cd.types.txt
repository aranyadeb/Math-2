Q: If a piecewise function is continuous at $x=0$ and a constant must be found
R: "$f(x)=\frac{\sin(p+1)x+\sin x}x$ for $x<0$, $q$ at $0$, $\frac{\sqrt{x+x^2}-\sqrt x}{x^{3/2}}$ for $x>0$", "$(1+ax)^{1/x}$, $1+b$, $\frac{\sqrt{x+4}-2}{(x+c)^{1/3}-2}$", "$f(x)=\frac{\cos(\sin x)-\cos x}{x^4}$; $f(0)=\frac1k$".
F: \text{continuous at } a\iff\lim_{x\to a^-}f=\lim_{x\to a^+}f=f(a)
S: Compute the left limit and the right limit separately (standard limits, series, rationalising). || Set both equal to the value at the point. || If one side has a form like $\frac{\sqrt{x+4}-2}{(x+c)^{1/3}-2}$, the denominator must also vanish at 0, which fixes $c$. || Solve for all constants.
E: Left limit $e^a$; right limit needs $c=8$ and equals $\frac{1/4}{1/12}=3$; so $1+b=3$, $e^a=3$: $e^{abc}=3^{16}$.
P: 03:2,3,14,17,31,15,23

Q: If continuity (and differentiability) must hold at several points of a piecewise function
R: "$\frac{a-b\cos2x}{x^2}$, $x^2+cx+2$, $2x+1$ continuous everywhere", "$ae^x+be^{-x}$, $cx^2$, $ax^2+2cx$ with $f'(0)$ given", "continuous at $x=-\frac32$ and $f\circ f(x)=\frac75$".
F: \text{one equation per joining point (value), plus one per point for differentiability (slope)}
S: List the joining points. || At each, equate the two neighbouring expressions (limits) — one equation each. || For differentiability, also equate the derivatives of the neighbouring pieces. || Solve the system; then answer (number of non-differentiable points, a value).
E: At $0$: $\frac{a-b\cos2x}{x^2}$ needs $a=b$ and limit $2a=2$, so $a=b=1$; at $1$: $3+c=3$, $c=0$.
P: 03:10,20,16,24,32

Q: If the number of points where $f$ is not differentiable is asked (moduli, max, min)
R: "$|x-1|\cos|x-2|\sin|x-1|+(x-3)|x^2-5x+4|$", "$|2-|x-3||$", "$\max\{|x|,x^2\}$ on $|x|\le2$, $8-2|x|$ beyond".
F: |g(x)|\ \text{has a corner where } g=0 \text{ and } g'\ne0;\ \ (x-a)|x-a|\ \text{is differentiable at } a
S: Find where each expression inside a modulus is zero. || At each such point, check whether the factor multiplying $|x-a|$ is non-zero there (then it is a corner) or zero (then smooth). || For max/min, sketch both graphs; corners appear where they cross with different slopes. || Count also the joining points of pieces where the slopes differ.
E: $|2-|x-3||$: corners at $x=3$ and $x=1,5$; $\sum f(f(x))=f(0)+f(2)+f(0)=3$.
P: 03:7,8,1

Q: If $[\ ]$ (greatest integer) causes discontinuities and they must be counted
R: "$[\frac{x^2}2]-[\sqrt x]$ on $[0,4]$", "$[4x^2-8x+5]$", "$h=\min\{\{x\},1-\{x\}\}$", "$[x^2]\sin\pi x$".
F: [g(x)] \text{ jumps where } g(x) \text{ crosses an integer}
S: Find all $x$ in the interval where the inside reaches an integer value. || At each, check whether the function actually jumps (two jumps can cancel; a factor like $\sin\pi x=0$ can remove it). || Check endpoints only from inside the interval. || Count.
E: Jumps of $[\frac{x^2}2]$ at $\sqrt2,2,\sqrt6,\ldots,4$ and of $[\sqrt x]$ at $1,4$; at $4$ both jump and cancel ⇒ 8 points.

Q: If $f(x+y)$ is given by a rule with $f$ and $f'$ (find $f$ by differentiating or substituting)
R: "$f(x+y)=f(x)f'(y)+f'(x)f(y)$, $f(0)=1$", "$f(x+y)=f(x)f(y)$, $f(1)=7$ and $g(x+y)=g(xy)$", "$f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)+1-\frac27xy$", "$(\sin x\cos y)(f(2x+2y)-f(2x-2y))=\ldots$".
F: f(x+y)=f(x)f(y)\Rightarrow f=a^x;\ \ \text{put } y=0 \text{ to get } f' \text{ in terms of } f
S: Put $y=0$ (and $x=y=0$) to get values like $f(0)$, $f'(0)$. || Differentiate with respect to $y$ and put $y=0$: this gives a differential equation $f'(x)=kf(x)$ etc. || Solve it with the initial value. || Use the closed form in the sum asked.
E: $y=0$: $f'(x)=f(x)(1-f'(0))$ and $f'(0)=\frac12$, so $f=e^{x/2}$; $\sum_{n=1}^{100}\ln f(n)=\frac{5050}2=2525$.
P: 03:11,12,13,36

Q: If the equation links $f(x)$ with $f(\frac kx)$ (or a polynomial satisfies $f(x)f(\frac1x)=f(x)+f(\frac1x)$)
R: "$3f(x)+2f(\frac m{19x})=5x$", "degree-2 polynomial with $f(x)f(\frac1x)=f(x)+f(\frac1x)$".
F: f(x)f(\tfrac1x)=f(x)+f(\tfrac1x)\Rightarrow f(x)=1\pm x^n
S: Replace $x$ by $\frac kx$ to get a second equation. || Solve the two linear equations for $f(x)$. || For the polynomial identity, use the known family $f=1\pm x^n$ and the given value or range to fix the sign.
E: $3f(x)+2f(\frac{15}x)=5x$ ⇒ $f(x)=3x-\frac{30}x$; $f(5)-f(2)=18$.

Q: If $f(2x)-f(x)=x$ or $f(x+1)=xf(x)$ type recurrences are given
R: "$f(2x)-f(x)=x$, $f(0)=1$; $G(x)=\lim\{f(x)-f(\frac x{2^n})\}$", "$f(x+1)=xf(x)$, $g=\ln f$; $g''(N+\frac12)-g''(\frac12)$".
F: f(x)-f\left(\frac x{2^n}\right)=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac x{2^k}\to x;\quad g(x+1)=\ln x+g(x)\Rightarrow g''(x+1)-g''(x)=-\frac1{x^2}
S: Replace $x$ by $\frac x2,\frac x4,\ldots$ and add the equations (telescoping). || Take the limit using continuity. || For $f(x+1)=xf(x)$, take logs and differentiate twice; then telescope over integer steps.
E: $G(x)=x$.

Q: If a limit is built from $f(a)$ and $f'(a)$ (or $f''$)
R: "$\lim_{x\to a}\frac{xf(a)-af(x)}{x-a}$ with $f(a)=4$, $f'(a)=2$", "$\lim_{x\to1}\left(\frac{f(2+x)}{f(3)}\right)^{\frac{18}{(x-1)^2}}$ with $f'(3)=0$, $f''(3)=4$".

S: Add and subtract $af(a)$ to split into a derivative form. || For $1^\infty$ forms, expand $f$ by Taylor's theorem around the point. || Substitute the given values.
E: $f(a)-af'(a)=4-2a$.
