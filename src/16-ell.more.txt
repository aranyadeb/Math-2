## Standard ellipse
Q: Eccentricity from axes
F: e=\sqrt{1-\frac{b^2}{a^2}}\quad(a>b)

Q: Ellipse from focus–directrix
F: SP=e\cdot PM,\ \ 0<e<1

Q: Shifted ellipse
F: \frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2}+\frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2}=1:\ \text{centre }(h,k),\ \text{foci }(h\pm ae,k)

Q: Distance between foci and directrices
F: 2ae\ \text{(foci)},\qquad\frac{2a}e\ \text{(directrices)}

Q: Ends of latus rectum
F: \left(\pm ae,\ \pm\frac{b^2}a\right)

Q: Ellipse in general second-degree form
F: h^2<ab\ \text{and}\ \Delta\ne0

## Chords
Q: Chord with eccentric angles and focal chord length
F: \text{focal chord at angle } \theta \text{ with major axis}: \ \frac{2ab^2}{a^2\sin^2\theta+b^2\cos^2\theta}

Q: Length of chord cut by $y=mx+c$
F: \frac{2ab\sqrt{1+m^2}\sqrt{a^2m^2+b^2-c^2}}{a^2m^2+b^2}

Q: Locus of midpoints of chords of given slope
F: y=-\frac{b^2}{a^2m}x\ \ (\text{a diameter})

## Tangents and normals
Q: Tangents from an external point (slopes)
F: (x_1^2-a^2)m^2-2x_1y_1m+(y_1^2-b^2)=0

Q: Point of intersection of tangents at $\alpha$, $\beta$
F: \left(\frac{a\cos\frac{\alpha+\beta}2}{\cos\frac{\alpha-\beta}2},\ \frac{b\sin\frac{\alpha+\beta}2}{\cos\frac{\alpha-\beta}2}\right)

Q: Tangent intercepts on axes
F: \frac{a^2}{x_1},\ \frac{b^2}{y_1};\qquad\text{min area of triangle with axes}=ab

Q: Minimum length of tangent intercept between axes
F: a+b

Q: Normal intercept on major axis
F: x=e^2x_1

Q: Number of normals from a point
F: \text{at most four};\ \text{from the centre: four (axes count)}

Q: Common tangent of two ellipses / ellipse and circle
F: \text{use } c^2=a^2m^2+b^2\ \text{for both and equate}

## Common results
Q: Auxiliary circle and eccentric angle relation
F: \tan\phi=\frac ba\tan\theta\ \ (\phi=\text{angle of } P \text{ from centre},\ \theta=\text{eccentric angle})

Q: Sum of squares of reciprocals of perpendicular semi-diameters
F: \frac1{r_1^2}+\frac1{r_2^2}=\frac1{a^2}+\frac1{b^2}

Q: Polar of a focus
F: \text{the corresponding directrix}

Q: Ellipse from a sliding ladder (trammel)
F: \text{point dividing a rod of length } a+b \text{ sliding on axes traces } \frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1

Q: Area of the parallelogram formed by tangents at ends of conjugate diameters
F: 4ab

Q: Distance from centre to tangent
F: p=\frac{ab}{\sqrt{a^2\sin^2\theta+b^2\cos^2\theta}}
