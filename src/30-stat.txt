## Central tendency
Q: Mean
F: \bar x=\frac{\sum x_i}n,\qquad\bar x=\frac{\sum f_ix_i}{\sum f_i}
N: Step-deviation: $\bar x=A+\frac{\sum f_iu_i}{\sum f_i}h$, $u_i=\frac{x_i-A}h$.

Q: Combined mean
F: \bar x=\frac{n_1\bar x_1+n_2\bar x_2}{n_1+n_2}

Q: Median of grouped data
F: M=l+\frac{\frac N2-C}{f}\times h

Q: Mode of grouped data
F: Z=l+\frac{f_1-f_0}{2f_1-f_0-f_2}\times h
N: Empirical: Mode $=3$ Median $-2$ Mean.

## Dispersion
Q: Mean deviation
F: \text{MD}(a)=\frac{\sum|x_i-a|}n
N: Mean deviation is least about the median.

Q: Variance and standard deviation
F: \sigma^2=\frac{\sum(x_i-\bar x)^2}n=\frac{\sum x_i^2}n-\bar x^2
N: Grouped: $\sigma^2=\frac{\sum f_ix_i^2}N-\bar x^2$; step deviation: $\sigma^2=h^2\left[\frac{\sum fu^2}N-\left(\frac{\sum fu}N\right)^2\right]$.

Q: Effect of change of origin and scale
F: y_i=ax_i+b:\ \ \bar y=a\bar x+b,\quad\sigma_y=|a|\sigma_x
N: Variance does not change when a constant is added to every value.

Q: Combined variance
F: \sigma^2=\frac{n_1(\sigma_1^2+d_1^2)+n_2(\sigma_2^2+d_2^2)}{n_1+n_2},\quad d_i=\bar x_i-\bar x

Q: Coefficient of variation
F: \text{CV}=\frac\sigma{\bar x}\times100
N: Lower CV means more consistent.

## Common results
Q: Variance of first n natural numbers
F: \sigma^2=\frac{n^2-1}{12}
N: Mean $=\frac{n+1}2$. For an AP with common difference $d$: $\sigma^2=\frac{d^2(n^2-1)}{12}$.

Q: Correcting wrong observations
F: \text{new }\sum x=\text{old}-\text{wrong}+\text{correct};\quad\text{same for }\sum x^2

Q: Range of variance
F: \sigma^2\le\frac{(\max-\min)^2}4

Q: Variance of two numbers
F: \sigma=\frac{|a-b|}2
