Q: If the orthocentre is given (or asked) together with sides or vertices
R: "Two sides are $3x-2y+6=0$, $4x+5y-20=0$ and the orthocentre is $(1,1)$; find the third side", "two vertices and the orthocentre given, find $C$", "orthocentre of $(1,2),(2,3),(3,1)$".
F: AH\perp BC,\ BH\perp CA,\ CH\perp AB\quad(\text{slopes: } m_1m_2=-1)
S: Find the vertex where two known sides meet. || The altitude from that vertex passes through $H$, so $BC\perp AH$: this fixes the slope of the third side. || Get a second condition from another altitude: $BH\perp AC$ gives $B$ on its known side. || To compute $H$ from three vertices, intersect two altitudes. || Right-angled triangle: $H$ is the right-angle vertex.
E: For $(1,2),(2,3),(3,1)$: altitudes $x+y=4$ and $x-2y+3=0$ meet at $H(\frac53,\frac73)$, so $\alpha+4\beta=11$, $4\alpha+\beta=9$: $x^2-20x+99=0$.
