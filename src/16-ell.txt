## Standard ellipse
Q: Standard ellipse ($a>b$)
F: \frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1,\quad b^2=a^2(1-e^2)
G: p=2*cos(t),1.2*sin(t),0,2*pi ; -3,3,-2,2
N: Foci $(\pm ae,0)$, directrices $x=\pm\frac ae$, latus rectum $\frac{2b^2}a$, major axis $2a$, minor axis $2b$.

Q: Ellipse with major axis along $y$ ($b>a$)
F: \frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1,\ b>a:\ a^2=b^2(1-e^2),\ \text{foci }(0,\pm be)

Q: Focal distances
F: SP=a-ex_1,\quad S'P=a+ex_1,\quad SP+S'P=2a

Q: Parametric point (eccentric angle)
F: (a\cos\theta,\ b\sin\theta)
N: Auxiliary circle $x^2+y^2=a^2$.

Q: Position of a point
F: S_1=\frac{x_1^2}{a^2}+\frac{y_1^2}{b^2}-1:\ <0\ \text{inside},\ =0\ \text{on},\ >0\ \text{outside}

## Chords
Q: Chord joining $\alpha,\beta$
F: \frac xa\cos\frac{\alpha+\beta}2+\frac yb\sin\frac{\alpha+\beta}2=\cos\frac{\alpha-\beta}2
N: Focal chord condition: $\tan\frac\alpha2\tan\frac\beta2=\frac{e-1}{e+1}$ (through $(ae,0)$).

Q: Chord with given midpoint, chord of contact, pair of tangents
F: T=S_1,\qquad T=0,\qquad SS_1=T^2

## Tangents and normals
Q: Tangent at $(x_1,y_1)$ and at $\theta$
F: \frac{xx_1}{a^2}+\frac{yy_1}{b^2}=1;\qquad\frac{x\cos\theta}a+\frac{y\sin\theta}b=1

Q: Slope form and tangency condition
F: y=mx\pm\sqrt{a^2m^2+b^2};\qquad c^2=a^2m^2+b^2
N: Point of contact $\left(\frac{\mp a^2m}{\sqrt{a^2m^2+b^2}},\frac{\pm b^2}{\sqrt{a^2m^2+b^2}}\right)$.

Q: Normal at $(x_1,y_1)$ and at $\theta$
F: \frac{a^2x}{x_1}-\frac{b^2y}{y_1}=a^2-b^2;\qquad ax\sec\theta-by\csc\theta=a^2-b^2

Q: Slope form of normal
F: y=mx\mp\frac{(a^2-b^2)m}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2m^2}}

Q: Director circle
F: x^2+y^2=a^2+b^2

Q: Reflection property
F: \text{the tangent and normal bisect the angles between the focal radii}

Q: Product of perpendiculars from foci to a tangent
F: p_1p_2=b^2
N: Foot of perpendicular from a focus to a tangent lies on the auxiliary circle.

## Common results
Q: Area of ellipse
F: A=\pi ab

Q: Conormal points
F: \text{up to 4 normals from a point};\ \ \alpha+\beta+\gamma+\delta=(2n+1)\pi
N: Eccentric angles of the feet of co-normal points.

Q: Concyclic points on an ellipse
F: \alpha+\beta+\gamma+\delta=2n\pi

Q: Conjugate diameters
F: m_1m_2=-\frac{b^2}{a^2};\quad\text{eccentric angles differ by }\tfrac\pi2;\quad CP^2+CD^2=a^2+b^2

Q: Locus of the point of intersection of perpendicular tangents
F: \text{director circle } x^2+y^2=a^2+b^2

Q: Maximum area of triangle inscribed / ellipse tricks
F: \text{max area of inscribed triangle}=\frac{3\sqrt3}4ab
N: Obtained from an equilateral triangle in the auxiliary circle compressed by $\frac ba$.

Q: Length of the latus rectum and semi-latus rectum
F: \frac{2b^2}a;\qquad\frac1{SP}+\frac1{SQ}=\frac{2a}{b^2}\ (\text{focal chord } PQ)
