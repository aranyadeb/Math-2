## If this type comes, solve like this
Q: If it asks the term independent of $x$ / a given power of $x$
F: T_{r+1}={}^nC_r\,(\text{first})^{n-r}(\text{second})^r;\ \ \text{set the power of } x\text{ equal to the target}
N: Write the power of $x$ in $T_{r+1}$ as a linear function of $r$, solve, check $r$ is an integer in $[0,n]$. With a polynomial multiplier $(1+2x-3x^3)(\ldots)^9$, add contributions for each multiplier term.

Q: If it asks the coefficient in a product of expansions
F: [x^k]\,(A(x)B(x))=\sum_ra_rb_{k-r}
N: Simplify first: $(1+x)(1-x)^{10}(1+x+x^2)^9=(1-x)(1-x^3)^9\cdot\ldots$; $(1-x)^{101}(1+x+x^2)^{100}=(1-x)(1-x^3)^{100}$.
P: 18:5,6,21,11,22

Q: If coefficients of consecutive terms are in a ratio / AP / GP
F: \frac{{}^nC_r}{{}^nC_{r-1}}=\frac{n-r+1}r
N: Two ratios ⇒ two linear equations in $n$ and $r$. AP: $2\,{}^nC_r={}^nC_{r-1}+{}^nC_{r+1}$.
P: 18:2,20,26,28,42,18,25,19

Q: If it counts rational/integral terms in $(a^{1/p}+b^{1/q})^n$

P: 18:8,9,31,32,33
