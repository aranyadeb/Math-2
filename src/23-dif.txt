## Standard derivatives
Q: Power, exponential, log
F: \frac{d}{dx}x^n=nx^{n-1},\quad\frac{d}{dx}e^x=e^x,\quad\frac{d}{dx}a^x=a^x\ln a,\quad\frac{d}{dx}\ln x=\frac1x
N: $\frac{d}{dx}\log_a x=\frac1{x\ln a}$; $\frac{d}{dx}\ln|x|=\frac1x$.

Q: Trigonometric derivatives
F: (\sin x)'=\cos x,\ (\cos x)'=-\sin x,\ (\tan x)'=\sec^2x,\ (\cot x)'=-\csc^2x,\ (\sec x)'=\sec x\tan x,\ (\csc x)'=-\csc x\cot x
D: $\frac{\sin(x+h)-\sin x}h=\frac{2\cos(x+\frac h2)\sin\frac h2}{h}\to\cos x$.

Q: Inverse trigonometric derivatives
F: (\sin^{-1}x)'=\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}},\ (\cos^{-1}x)'=-\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}},\ (\tan^{-1}x)'=\frac1{1+x^2}
N: $(\cot^{-1}x)'=-\frac1{1+x^2}$, $(\sec^{-1}x)'=\frac1{|x|\sqrt{x^2-1}}$, $(\csc^{-1}x)'=-\frac1{|x|\sqrt{x^2-1}}$.

Q: Derivative of $|x|$
F: \frac{d}{dx}|x|=\frac{|x|}x=\frac x{|x|},\quad x\ne0

## Rules
Q: Product and quotient rules
F: (uv)'=u'v+uv',\qquad\left(\frac uv\right)'=\frac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}
N: $(uvw)'=u'vw+uv'w+uvw'$.

Q: Chain rule
F: \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy}{du}\cdot\frac{du}{dx}

Q: Parametric differentiation
F: \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt},\qquad\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)\Big/\frac{dx}{dt}

Q: Implicit differentiation
F: F(x,y)=0\Rightarrow\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{F_x}{F_y}

Q: Logarithmic differentiation
F: y=u^v\Rightarrow\frac{dy}{dx}=u^v\left(v'\ln u+\frac{vu'}u\right)

Q: Derivative of inverse function
F: \frac{dx}{dy}=\frac1{dy/dx},\qquad\frac{d^2x}{dy^2}=-\frac{d^2y/dx^2}{(dy/dx)^3}

Q: Leibniz rule for the n-th derivative of a product
F: (uv)^{(n)}=\sum_{r=0}^n{}^nC_r\,u^{(n-r)}v^{(r)}

Q: Differentiation under the integral sign (Leibniz)
F: \frac{d}{dx}\int_{g(x)}^{h(x)}f(t)\,dt=f(h(x))h'(x)-f(g(x))g'(x)

## n-th derivatives
Q: Standard n-th derivatives
F: \frac{d^n}{dx^n}e^{ax}=a^ne^{ax},\quad\frac{d^n}{dx^n}\sin(ax+b)=a^n\sin\left(ax+b+\frac{n\pi}2\right)
N: $\frac{d^n}{dx^n}\frac1{ax+b}=\frac{(-1)^nn!\,a^n}{(ax+b)^{n+1}}$; $\frac{d^n}{dx^n}\ln(ax+b)=\frac{(-1)^{n-1}(n-1)!\,a^n}{(ax+b)^n}$.

## Common results
Q: Substitution tricks
F: \sqrt{1-x^2}\to x=\sin\theta;\quad1+x^2\to x=\tan\theta;\quad\frac{1-x}{1+x}\to x=\cos2\theta
N: E.g. $\frac{d}{dx}\tan^{-1}\frac{2x}{1-x^2}=\frac2{1+x^2}$ for $|x|<1$.

Q: Infinite radical / power towers
F: y=\sqrt{f+\sqrt{f+\cdots}}\Rightarrow y^2=f+y\Rightarrow\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{f'}{2y-1}
N: $y=x^{x^{x^{\cdots}}}\Rightarrow y=x^y\Rightarrow\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{y^2}{x(1-y\ln x)}$.

Q: Derivative of one function w.r.t. another
F: \frac{du}{dv}=\frac{du/dx}{dv/dx}

Q: Derivative of the inverse function at a point
F: g=f^{-1}\Rightarrow g'(f(a))=\frac1{f'(a)}
