## Points and direction ratios
Q: Distance and section formula in 3D
F: d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2+(z_2-z_1)^2};\qquad\left(\frac{mx_2+nx_1}{m+n},\dots\right)

Q: Direction ratios of the line through two points
F: (x_2-x_1,\ y_2-y_1,\ z_2-z_1)
N: Direction cosines $=\frac{\text{d.r.}}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}$.

Q: Angle between two lines
F: \cos\theta=\frac{a_1a_2+b_1b_2+c_1c_2}{\sqrt{a_1^2+b_1^2+c_1^2}\sqrt{a_2^2+b_2^2+c_2^2}}
N: Perpendicular: $a_1a_2+b_1b_2+c_1c_2=0$; parallel: $\frac{a_1}{a_2}=\frac{b_1}{b_2}=\frac{c_1}{c_2}$.

Q: Projection of a segment on a line
F: (x_2-x_1)l+(y_2-y_1)m+(z_2-z_1)n

## Lines
Q: Equation of a line
F: \vec r=\vec a+\lambda\vec b;\qquad\frac{x-x_1}{a}=\frac{y-y_1}{b}=\frac{z-z_1}{c}
N: Two-point form: $\frac{x-x_1}{x_2-x_1}=\frac{y-y_1}{y_2-y_1}=\frac{z-z_1}{z_2-z_1}$.

Q: Shortest distance between skew lines
F: d=\frac{|(\vec a_2-\vec a_1)\cdot(\vec b_1\times\vec b_2)|}{|\vec b_1\times\vec b_2|}
N: Lines intersect iff $(\vec a_2-\vec a_1)\cdot(\vec b_1\times\vec b_2)=0$ (and not parallel).

Q: Distance between parallel lines
F: d=\frac{|(\vec a_2-\vec a_1)\times\vec b|}{|\vec b|}

Q: Perpendicular distance of a point from a line
F: d=\frac{|(\vec p-\vec a)\times\vec b|}{|\vec b|}
N: Foot: take a general point $\vec a+\lambda\vec b$ and use $(\text{foot}-\vec p)\cdot\vec b=0$.

Q: Coplanarity of two lines
F: \begin{vmatrix}x_2-x_1&y_2-y_1&z_2-z_1\\a_1&b_1&c_1\\a_2&b_2&c_2\end{vmatrix}=0

## Planes
Q: Equation of a plane
F: \vec r\cdot\hat n=d;\qquad ax+by+cz+d=0;\qquad\frac xa+\frac yb+\frac zc=1
N: $(a,b,c)$ is normal to $ax+by+cz+d=0$. Normal form: $lx+my+nz=p$.

Q: Plane through a point with a given normal
F: a(x-x_1)+b(y-y_1)+c(z-z_1)=0

Q: Plane through three points
F: \begin{vmatrix}x-x_1&y-y_1&z-z_1\\x_2-x_1&y_2-y_1&z_2-z_1\\x_3-x_1&y_3-y_1&z_3-z_1\end{vmatrix}=0

Q: Distance of a point from a plane
F: d=\frac{|ax_1+by_1+cz_1+d|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}

Q: Distance between parallel planes
F: d=\frac{|d_1-d_2|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}

Q: Angle between two planes
F: \cos\theta=\frac{|a_1a_2+b_1b_2+c_1c_2|}{\sqrt{\sum a_1^2}\sqrt{\sum a_2^2}}

Q: Angle between a line and a plane
F: \sin\phi=\frac{|al+bm+cn|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}\sqrt{l^2+m^2+n^2}}
N: Line $\parallel$ plane iff $al+bm+cn=0$; line $\perp$ plane iff $\frac la=\frac mb=\frac nc$.

Q: Family of planes through a line of intersection
F: P_1+\lambda P_2=0

Q: Foot of perpendicular and image in a plane
F: \frac{x-x_1}a=\frac{y-y_1}b=\frac{z-z_1}c=-\frac{ax_1+by_1+cz_1+d}{a^2+b^2+c^2}\ (\text{foot});\ \ \times2\ \text{for image}

Q: Bisector planes
F: \frac{a_1x+b_1y+c_1z+d_1}{\sqrt{a_1^2+b_1^2+c_1^2}}=\pm\frac{a_2x+b_2y+c_2z+d_2}{\sqrt{a_2^2+b_2^2+c_2^2}}

Q: Plane containing a line and a point / two lines
F: \text{normal}=\vec b_1\times\vec b_2\ \text{or}\ \vec b\times(\vec p-\vec a)

Q: Line of intersection of two planes
F: \text{direction}=\vec n_1\times\vec n_2;\ \text{point: put } z=0\ \text{and solve}

## Common results
Q: Volume of a tetrahedron
F: V=\frac16\left|\begin{vmatrix}x_2-x_1&y_2-y_1&z_2-z_1\\x_3-x_1&y_3-y_1&z_3-z_1\\x_4-x_1&y_4-y_1&z_4-z_1\end{vmatrix}\right|

Q: Area of the triangle cut by a plane from the axes
F: \frac xa+\frac yb+\frac zc=1:\ \ \Delta=\tfrac12\sqrt{a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2}
N: Volume of the tetrahedron with the origin $=\frac{|abc|}6$.

Q: Projections of an area
F: A^2=A_{xy}^2+A_{yz}^2+A_{zx}^2

Q: Sphere (for reference)
F: |\vec r-\vec c|=R;\qquad x^2+y^2+z^2+2ux+2vy+2wz+d=0,\ R=\sqrt{u^2+v^2+w^2-d}

Q: Angle between a diagonal of a cube and an edge / face diagonal
F: \cos^{-1}\tfrac1{\sqrt3}\ (\text{with edge}),\qquad\cos^{-1}\sqrt{\tfrac23}\ (\text{with face diagonal})
N: Angle between two body diagonals $=\cos^{-1}\frac13$.
